# Social preview

Specification for the repository's social preview image (Settings → Social preview).

- **Size:** 1280 × 640 px, PNG, under 1 MB. Keep content inside a 1200 × 560 safe area.
- **Background:** near-black (`#0B0B0C`). Alternative light version: off-white (`#F5F5F2`) with near-black text.
- **Text, left-aligned, vertically centered:**
  - `PRAXIA` in small caps, letter-spaced (~0.3 em), 28 px, 60 % grey
  - `Agent Core` in a geometric or grotesk sans (e.g. Inter, Geist), 112 px, white, tight tracking
  - `Event → Agent → Action.` in a monospace (e.g. JetBrains Mono, Geist Mono), 36 px, 70 % grey
- **Graphic (optional, right third):** a thin-line flow of eight small nodes (Event → … → Result) as 1.5 px outlined rectangles connected by straight arrows, one node (Approval) offset below the line. Monochrome only.
- **Avoid:** robots, brains, glowing neon, gradients, stock imagery, more than one accent tone.
- **Accessibility:** text contrast at least 7:1. No essential information only in the graphic.
